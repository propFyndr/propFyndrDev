# RealtyAsk: This Week's Plan (15 Oct – 21 Oct 2026)

**Rule for the week:** no new features. We make what already exists faster, more honest and cheaper to run.

**How to read this:** each day has 5 tasks. Each task has a clear result ("Result") so anyone can tell when it is done.

---

## Day 1 — Thu 15 Oct: Separate live and dev environments

1. **Move the live database from Supabase to Neon.** Real buyers' data now sits on Neon, in Singapore next to our app server. *Result:* live runs on its own dedicated database.
2. **Keep Supabase as our dev environment.** Dev gets its own database, separate from live. *Result:* the whole app can be tested end to end on dev without using up live's database space, server hours or AI budget, and a test can never touch a real buyer's data.
3. **Give dev its own short-term memory (cache).** Dev and live used to share one, so test data could leak into live answers. *Result:* the two environments are fully separate.
4. **Shrink what each chat stores.** About 60% of the old database was full property cards saved with every message. We now save the small version and fetch the full card when needed. *Result:* the free plan would have filled in about 2 months; now it lasts.
5. **Set up company email on Zoho Mail.** Addresses like hello@ on our own domain, with automatic emails (invites) sent separately. *Result:* invitations and replies come from a trusted company address.

## Day 2 — Fri 16 Oct: Learn from the best assistants

1. **Collect 10 real buyer questions.** Budget, possession, metro, builder trust, total cost. *Result:* a saved list the team agrees on.
2. **Record how ChatGPT and one other assistant answer them.** Same method as last time: copy the answer data from the browser's Network tab. *Result:* two reference answers for each question.
3. **Score our answers against theirs.** One line per question: where we are weaker (slower, shorter, no reason given, no next step). *Result:* a gap list ranked by how many buyers it affects.
4. **Fix the top 3 gaps.** Changes to how we already answer, nothing new. *Result:* the same 10 questions re-run and compared.
5. **Understand Hindi and Hinglish as well as English.** "3 BHK chahiye, budget 1.5 crore" must work like the English version. *Result:* 10 Hinglish questions pass.

## Day 3 — Sat 17 Oct: Remove every number we cannot prove

1. **Property page cost sheet reads real data.** Some charges there are placeholders. *Result:* every charge comes from the project's own cost sheet, or says "not available".
2. **Affordability answer stops guessing.** It must not assume a ₹1.5 Cr price or a loan rate without saying so. *Result:* every assumption is labelled as one.
3. **Clear unsourced legal and safety data.** On about 129 projects, details such as lift compliance and water quality were filled in by a script, not a source. We back up first, then blank them. *Result:* a backup file, and no unsourced value shown to buyers.
4. **Fix the comparison screen.** It must not show "high confidence" on estimated numbers. *Result:* estimates are labelled as estimates.
5. **Fix the safety test that passes when it finds nothing.** *Result:* the test fails when it should.

## Day 4 — Sun 18 Oct: See what buyers do

1. **Connect the AI monitoring tool (Langfuse) with the new keys.** Set them on live and dev, and measure how long a chat takes to appear there. *Result:* a live chat visible in Langfuse, with the delay noted.
2. **Build one buyer-journey view.** Chat started, project saved, callback requested, site visit requested, dossier shared. *Result:* one screen showing where buyers drop off.
3. **Test referral tracking end to end.** Share a link, open it as a new person, check the referral is credited once. *Result:* pass or a list of fixes.
4. **Read 20 real chats.** Mark every wrong, slow or unhelpful answer. *Result:* a list of problems with the chat text attached.
5. **Remove the analytics key typed into the code.** *Result:* the key lives only in settings.

## Day 5 — Mon 19 Oct: Faster answers, lower AI cost

1. **Measure speed.** Time 30 typical questions. *Result:* typical and slowest times recorded as the baseline.
2. **Measure how often we already reuse answers.** We already save answers for repeated questions. *Result:* a hit-rate number (how many questions were answered without calling the AI).
3. **Reuse answers for similar questions, not only identical ones.** If one buyer asks about a 3 BHK in Sector 150 under ₹1.5 Cr and another asks the same in different words, the second gets the saved answer. We match on what the question *means* (sector, size, budget), not the exact words, and only for factual questions. *Result:* a higher hit-rate on the same 30 questions, with zero wrong matches.
4. **Answer more facts straight from our data.** Possession, RERA and payment plan need no AI. *Result:* a list of fact questions that still call the AI, and the fixes.
5. **Make rate limits see the real buyer.** Today they may see our hosting company's address. *Result:* one heavy user cannot block everyone.

2. **Write a one-page "how RealtyAsk works".** Buyer app, admin, portals, in plain language. *Result:* a page the whole team can read.
3. **Update the project records (MEMORY.md and docs).** *Result:* they match what is live.
4. **Switch visible old brand names to RealtyAsk.** Emails and settings still say propfyndr in places. *Result:* buyers and staff see only RealtyAsk.
5. **Check all five logins** (Super Admin, Analyst, Sales, Builder, Partner). *Result:* all five pass.

## Day 7 — Wed 21 Oct: Prove it works

1. **Run the full chat test (about 60 questions) on the live AI.** *Result:* a scorecard saved in the repo.
2. **Test as a buyer on a phone.** Chat, save, callback, share dossier. *Result:* each step passes or is listed as a bug.
3. **Test the builder side.** A lead arrives with its brief and no chat transcript. *Result:* confirmed.
4. **Fix what the week turned up.** Bugs only. *Result:* the bug list closed.
5. **Write the weekly report.** *Result:* done, not done, and next steps in one page.
