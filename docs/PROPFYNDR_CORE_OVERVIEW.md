## 1. What is PropFyndr?

**PropFyndr** is an **intelligent real estate advisor** designed specifically for home buyers in **Noida, Greater Noida, and the Yamuna Expressway corridor**.

Traditional property portals (like 99acres or MagicBricks) are advertising billboards that push sponsored listings and sell user phone numbers to cold-calling brokers. 

**PropFyndr works like an objective, trusted private consultant**:
* It chats with the home buyer in plain English.
* It answers deep, high-stakes questions about budget, floor plans, legal safety, and hidden costs.
* It recommends homes based strictly on data and buyer fit, not broker commissions.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy: "Zero Bullsh*t, Verified Facts Only"

The single foundational rule across the platform: **The AI must never guess, invent numbers, or lie.**

In real estate, being wrong about a payment schedule, price, or possession date by even 5% can cost a family lakhs of rupees.

* **When data is verified**: The platform gives exact, authoritative numbers (RERA registration, actual builder payment plans, maintenance costs, and carpet area).
* **When data is missing or unconfirmed**: Instead of letting the AI hallucinate a plausible guess, the system transparently admits it:  
  > *"We don't have the developer's officially verified payment schedule for this project yet, so we won't guess. Here is what is standard for this sector..."*

## 3. The 3 Big Problems It Solves for Buyers

### 1. The "True Cost" Trap (Hidden Charges)
Builders advertise an attractive base rate (e.g., ₹7,000/sq.ft), but the buyer ends up paying for parking, club memberships, power backup, floor rise, PLC (park-facing fees), GST, and stamp duty.  
**PropFyndr calculates the true, out-of-pocket, all-inclusive price** upfront.

### 2. Builder Reliability Risk
Many projects in NCR have suffered from multi-year construction delays.  
**PropFyndr tracks a builder score for every developer** based on actual past deliveries, delivery delays, financial strength, and pending litigations.

### 3. Information Overload & Sales Pressure
Instead of decoding 40-page marketing brochures or dealing with pushy sales calls, buyers can ask direct questions:
* *"Which 3 BHK in Sector 150 has the highest carpet efficiency under ₹2.5 Cr?"*
* *"What is the exact payment schedule for ATS Pristine?"*
* *"Is Sector 150 safe from flooding, and what is the water source?"*

## 4. How the App Works (The User Experience)

1. **Conversational Advisory**: The buyer chats naturally. The AI understands local sectors, metro routes, highway connections, and micro-market nuances.
2. **Project DNA (0–100 Scores)**: Every society is evaluated across 6 key pillars:
   * **Builder Reliability** (Track record and delivery history)
   * **Price Value** (Fair valuation relative to the sector)
   * **Location & Connectivity** (Commute times to tech hubs, schools, and airports)
   * **Legal & RERA Compliance** (Occupancy Certificate status and registry safety)
   * **Amenities & Green Space** (Open spaces, club size, sports courts)
   * **Possession Certainty** (Delivered vs timeline risk)
3. **Smart One-Tap Action Chips**: At every step, the app displays interactive suggestion buttons (e.g., *"Calculate Monthly EMI"*, *"Compare with neighboring projects"*, *"View Cost Sheet"*).
4. **Built-in Financial Calculators**: Real-time calculation of UP stamp duty (7%), registration (1%), 0% GST on ready homes, and bank loan EMIs.
