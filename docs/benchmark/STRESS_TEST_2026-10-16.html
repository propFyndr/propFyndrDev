Where the chat lets the buyer down

  1. Follow-ups about a project fall through to the AI. "Is there any litigation on this project?", "how has the price moved?", "what's the catch?" and "EMI with 20% down" all have answers in our rows or our calculators. All four went to the AI path and failed with the outage message (4 of the 6 outages).
  2. Questions answered generically when we hold the answer. "Has the OC been given?" (the row says yes) and "What are the payment options?" for Logix (a down-payment plan is on record).
  3. A Hinglish follow-up drops the search. After "2BHK, Greater Noida West, 70 lakh", "Ready to move hona chahiye" listed under-construction projects of every size.
  4. Open questions get a data table. "Where do I even start?" and "areas good for a family with kids" both got a micro-market table that is mostly "Not recorded".
  5. "What are you assuming about me?" returns a cut-off quote of the first message, not the assumptions.
  6. Market figures without a label. "5–7% a year, 8–12% near Jewar", "loan of ₹35–40 lakh", "25–35% loading" and NBCC figures were given with no source or date.
  7. Leftover text. "They're on the cards above" appeared 4 times with no cards.

What held up

  1. Other city, rent, prompt injection, heavy typos, an impossible budget, nonsense and "give me a number" were all handled honestly.
  2. Through three "by the way" detours, the search (3 BHK, Sector 150, under 2 Cr) was remembered and restated, and "make it 2.3 crore" was applied.
  3. "Can I trust Logix?" now leads with Logix's own record: 24 months' delay, NCLT proceedings.
  4. A passed possession date is said plainly: "The status is unconfirmed, as the possession date has passed."
