Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite took 17–20 s to its first token (direct curl, 26 Oct), and the chain gives each leg 8 s. The four Gemini legs use about 32 s of the 35 s budget, and the other providers never start.

**What the fix changed (run 1 → 3):** 23 "best …" turns had code-built cards or a ranking table on screen, then the outage notice. Now the buyer keeps those, with no notice. These turns grade `too_short` (a one-line close under the cards). The next change (run 4, unit-tested, not re-run) writes the same row-based summary a plain search gets.

**Still failing with the model down:** 4 open questions with nothing to show from rows: "are all properties in noida leasehold", "are there freehold properties", "best investment properties in noida / sector 107".

**Honest `too_short`:** "1 bhk flats in sector 75" and "2 bhk … sector 74 75 76" say we hold no match and offer to widen. That is correct, not a failure.
