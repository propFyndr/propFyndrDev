# RealtyAsk: This Week's Plan (27 Oct – 2 Nov 2026)

**Rule for the week:** every number a buyer sees must be real, and every answer must arrive. We finish what last week found before we start anything new.

**How to read this:** each day has 5 tasks. Each task has a clear result ("Result") so anyone can tell when it is done. A task marked *(owner decides)* needs a yes or no from Furqan before the work that depends on it can start.

**Where we start from:** last week's 60-question test scored 42 out of 60. Most failures were not wrong answers but no answer: our main AI service now takes 17–20 seconds to start replying, and we give up after 8. About 1 in 7 live answers was an "AI unavailable" message. Separately, in our test copy of the data, 754 of 1,021 flat prices were written by a script, not given by a builder (live has not been checked yet).

## Day 3 — Thu 29 Oct: Only real prices

1. **Back up and hide the script-written prices on dev**, as decided on Day 1. *Result:* a backup file, and the 310 affected projects show "price on request" instead of an invented figure.
2. **Collect real prices for the 30 projects buyers ask about most.** From the builders' own price lists, each with the date it was given. *Result:* 30 projects with a real, dated price.
3. **Show buyers how fresh a price is.** *Result:* every price answer says "confirmed by the builder on <date>", or "date not on record".

5. **Check live for script-written prices, then repeat steps 1–3 there.** *Result:* no script-written price on live; the 30 real prices are visible to buyers.

## Day 4 — Fri 30 Oct: Builders keep their own prices up to date

1. **A builder can propose a new price or mark flats as sold** from their portal. A proposal never changes the live site by itself. *Result:* a builder's proposal appears in our admin; the buyer still sees the old price.
2. **We approve or reject each proposal.** On approval the price goes live with the date and the name of who confirmed it. On rejection the builder sees why. *Result:* one approved and one rejected proposal, each with its reason on record.
3. **Every price change keeps its history.** *Result:* for any price, we can answer "who said this, and when".
4. **Old prices are flagged as old.** A price not confirmed for 30 days, or stock not confirmed for 7, is shown with its date and never as current. *Result:* a test project with a 31-day-old price shows the warning.
5. **Try it with one real builder.** *Result:* one real builder updates one real price from their portal, and it reaches buyers after our approval.

## Day 5 — Sat 31 Oct: Better leads for builders

1. **Note what holds each buyer back** (possession worry, price, builder history), as decided on Day 1. Competitor names are never passed on. *Result:* a test lead's brief shows the buyer's concerns, with no competitor named.
2. **Email the brief to the builder the moment a lead arrives.** *Result:* a test lead reaches the builder's inbox within a minute, with no chat transcript attached.
3. **Keep test leads out of builders' lists.** A test callback from last week is still showing in one builder's list. *Result:* builders see only real buyers.
4. **Make rate limits see the real buyer.** Today they may see our hosting company's address instead (left over from last week). *Result:* one heavy user cannot block everyone.
5. **Track each lead from brief to booking.** Brief opened, buyer called, site visit, booking. *Result:* one screen per builder showing how far each of their leads got.
