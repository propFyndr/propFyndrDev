# RealtyAsk: This Week's Plan (27 Oct – 2 Nov 2026)

**Rule for the week:** every buyer gets an answer, and every number they see is real.

**How to read this:** each day has 5 tasks. Each task has a clear result ("Result") so anyone can tell when it is done.

**Where we start:** last week's 60-question test scored 42 of 60. About 1 in 7 buyers saw "AI unavailable", and 754 of 1,021 flat prices were made up by a script.

**Choices this plan assumes** (Furqan confirms on Tuesday morning):
- When the main AI stalls, go straight to the backup.
- Hide made-up prices until a builder gives a real one.
- Record what holds a buyer back for the builder.
- Answer off-topic questions in one line, then steer back to property.

---

## Day 1 — Tue 27 Oct: Every question gets an answer

1. **Stop the AI from stalling.** If the main AI hasn't started replying within 8 seconds, switch to the backup. *Result:* a forced stall still gets an answer within 20 seconds.
2. **Count how often buyers see "AI unavailable".** Ask the same 30 questions three times, before and after the fix. *Result:* both rates recorded; target under 1 in 50.
3. **Re-run the 60-question test.** Same questions as last week, on the live AI. *Result:* a new scorecard saved in the repo, compared question by question with last week's 42.
4. **Reconnect Langfuse and add a failure alert.** New access key for our tools. *Result:* live chats searchable; the team is alerted when over 5% of answers fail in an hour.
5. **Reset the referral tree to one starting link, "Furqan Arth".** Clear old links on dev, then live. *Result:* a five-person test chain credits every chat, save and callback correctly.

## Day 2 — Wed 28 Oct: Find and hide every made-up price

1. **Count the made-up prices on live.** Use the same check that found 754 of 1,021 on dev. *Result:* the live count, plus the list of affected projects.
2. **Back up every price and payment plan on dev and live.** *Result:* two backup files saved, and one test restore on dev that brings everything back exactly.
3. **Hide made-up prices on dev.** Buyers see "price on request" and are offered a callback instead. *Result:* none of the 310 affected projects shows an invented price anywhere.
4. **Hide the copied payment plans.** 608 of 620 are one template copied across projects. *Result:* buyers see only real payment plans, or "not on record".
5. **Repeat the hiding on live** once the dev check passes. *Result:* no made-up price or payment plan appears anywhere on the live site, in chat or on property pages.

## Day 3 — Thu 29 Oct: Put real prices back

1. **List the 30 projects buyers ask about most.** Taken from the last 30 days of real chats in Langfuse. *Result:* a ranked list of 30 projects.
2. **Get each project's current price list from its builder.** PDF or email, each with the date given. *Result:* 30 dated price lists in one shared folder.
3. **Enter the 30 prices with their source and date.** *Result:* those 30 projects show a real price again, in chat and on the property page.
4. **Show buyers how fresh each price is.** *Result:* every price answer says "confirmed by the builder on <date>", and no price appears without a date.
5. **Warn when a price is old.** Prices unconfirmed for 30 days show their date and a warning. *Result:* a test price dated 31 days ago shows the warning.

## Day 4 — Fri 30 Oct: Builders keep their own prices up to date

1. **Add a "change price" form to the builder portal.** Nothing goes live by itself. *Result:* a test proposal appears in our admin while buyers still see the old price.
2. **Add an approve / reject screen to our admin.** Approval publishes the price with date and approver; rejection returns a reason. *Result:* one approved and one rejected proposal on record.
3. **Let builders mark a flat type as sold out**, through the same approval step. *Result:* once approved, the sold-out flat type is no longer offered to buyers.
4. **Keep the history of every price change.** *Result:* for any price, our admin shows who gave it and when, back to the first recorded price.
5. **Run it with one real builder.** *Result:* one real builder changes one real price from their portal, and buyers see it after our approval.

## Day 5 — Sat 31 Oct: Better leads for builders

1. **Record what holds each buyer back.** Save concerns like possession date, price or builder record on the lead. *Result:* a test brief lists concerns and names no competitor.
2. **Tell the builder the moment a lead arrives.** The email carries a link to the brief, never the buyer's details. *Result:* a test lead's email arrives within one minute.
3. **Keep test leads out of builders' lists.** One test callback from last week still shows for ACE Group. *Result:* builders see only real buyers, never test leads.
4. **Make rate limits see the real buyer**, not our hosting company's address. *Result:* one person sending too many messages is slowed down without blocking anyone else, tested from two phones.
5. **Track each lead from brief to booking.** Brief opened, buyer called, site visit, booking. *Result:* one screen per builder showing how far each lead got.

## Day 6 — Sun 1 Nov: The buyer's side, on a phone

1. **Test as a buyer on a real phone.** Chat, save, callback, site visit and share dossier. *Result:* each step passes, or is listed as a bug with repeat steps.
2. **Remember what the buyer said earlier.** A loan rate given once is forgotten by the next EMI question. *Result:* 10 two-step money questions keep rate, budget and size.
3. **Change a requirement without starting over.** "Actually make that 2 crore" re-runs the search with the new budget. *Result:* 10 different ways of saying it all work.
4. **Answer "can I trust this builder?" the same in Hindi and English.** *Result:* matching answers in both languages for 5 builders, checked side by side.
5. **Handle off-topic questions in one line.** Jokes, IPOs, other cities: answer briefly, then ask a property question. *Result:* 10 off-topic test questions all get this reply.

## Day 7 — Mon 2 Nov: Prove it works

1. **Run the 60-question test on the live AI.** Same questions as Day 1. *Result:* a scorecard saved in the repo, with a target of 54 of 60 or better.
2. **Read every real buyer chat from this week in Langfuse.** *Result:* a list of wrong, slow or unhelpful answers, each with its chat text attached.
3. **Fix what the week turned up.** Bugs only, no new features. *Result:* every bug is fixed, tested and closed, or moved to next week with a reason.
4. **Check all five logins:** Super Admin, Analyst, Sales, Builder and Partner. *Result:* each one signs in and sees only its own screens, written down.
5. **Write the weekly report.** *Result:* one page covering what was done, what was not, and next week's priorities, shared with the team.
