Before you sign a builder‑buyer agreement in Noida, verify the legal title, RERA registration, and core contract clauses. These checks protect your investment and clarify the builder’s obligations.

## Verify Legal Title and Ownership  

The first step is to obtain a **title search report** that confirms the builder actually owns the land or holds a clear lease. Ask for the ownership documents and check for any court cases or liens that could affect the project. <span href="https://www.toihomes.com/blogs/articles/regulatory/builder-buyer-agreement/134315931">title search report</span>, <span href="https://www.toihomes.com/blogs/articles/regulatory/builder-buyer-agreement/134315931">ownership documents</span>, and <span href="https://www.toihomes.com/blogs/articles/regulatory/builder-buyer-agreement/134315931">court cases or liens</span> are standard requests in Delhi‑NCR micro‑markets, including Noida.  

If the builder cannot provide clear title, the risk of future litigation or loss of possession rises sharply. **Do not proceed until the title is unequivocally clean**.

## Confirm Regulatory Compliance  

All ongoing projects in Uttar Pradesh must be registered with the Real Estate Regulatory Authority (*RERA*). Verify the RERA registration number on the agreement and cross‑check it on the official portal. <span href="https://www.toihomes.com/blogs/articles/regulatory/builder-buyer-agreement/134315931">RERA registration</span> is the foundation of a legitimate investment.  

Additionally, the agreement should contain a clause obligating the builder to hand over the *completion certificate* once construction is finished. <span href="https://www.commercialnoida.com/blog/what-is-builder-buyer-agreement.html">completion certificate clause</span> ensures you receive a legally recognized, habitable unit.

## Scrutinize Core Contract Clauses  

A robust agreement lists:

1. **Accurate personal and project details** – name, address, project name, and unit number. <span href="https://moneytreerealty.com/blog/what-is-a-builder-buyer-agreement-in-real-estate">personal and project details</span>  
2. **Fixed possession date** – the exact day the builder promises to hand over the unit. <span href="https://camantra.com/which-points-to-kept-in-mind-while-drafting-builder-buyer-agreement">fixed possession date</span>  
3. **Compensation for delay** – a predefined penalty if the builder misses the possession date. <span href="https://camantra.com/which-points-to-kept-in-mind-while-drafting-builder-buyer-agreement">compensation clause</span>  
4. **Payment schedule** – clear milestones tied to construction progress, not arbitrary dates.  
5. **Hidden clauses** – watch for terms that limit your right to cancel, impose excessive maintenance fees, or shift risk to you after handover. <span href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/manindar-verma-310062322_7-hidden-builder-agreement-clauses-every-activity-7478011309448466434-Xezs">hidden clauses</span>  

**Missing or vague language in any of these areas can become a source of dispute later**.

## Assess Financial and Post‑Purchase Obligations  

Beyond the purchase price, budget for registration fees, GST, and future maintenance charges. The agreement should spell out who bears each cost and when they become payable. <span href="https://www.toihomes.com/blogs/articles/regulatory/builder-buyer-agreement/134315931">post‑purchase costs</span>  

Understanding these obligations prevents surprise outlays after you take possession.

## What to check before you decide  

- Obtain a **title search report** and verify ownership documents.  
- Confirm the project’s **RERA registration number** on the official portal.  
- Ensure a **fixed possession date** and a clear **delay compensation** clause.  
- Look for a clause obligating the builder to provide a **completion certificate**.  
- Review the **payment schedule** and identify any **hidden cancellation or maintenance clauses**.  
- Calculate all **post‑purchase costs** (registration, GST, maintenance) and confirm who pays them.

## Frequently asked questions  

### Can I negotiate the possession date?  
Yes. The possession date is a contractual term, and you can request a specific date or a tighter penalty for delay before signing.

### What if the builder defaults on the title?  
If the title is later found to be disputed, you may face legal battles that can delay or even cancel possession. That’s why a clean title search is essential.

### Do I need a lawyer to review the agreement?  
Reading the agreement three times—alone, with a trusted person, and with a lawyer—is recommended to catch ambiguous or unfavorable clauses. <span href="https://www.toihomes.com/blogs/articles/regulatory/builder-buyer-agreement/134315931">lawyer review</span>

### How does RERA protect me?  
RERA registration obliges the builder to disclose project details, adhere to timelines, and provide a grievance redressal mechanism, offering a statutory safety net. <span href="https://www.toihomes.com/blogs/articles/regulatory/builder-buyer-agreement/134315931">RERA compliance</span>
