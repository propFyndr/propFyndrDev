# Top Queries & Distant Keywords: Delhi NCR & Noida Real Estate Intelligence

> **Document Scope:** This dossier expands into the advanced, regulatory, geo-environmental, institutional, and post-possession dimensions of real estate across Delhi NCR, Noida, Greater Noida West, and the Yamuna Expressway (YEIDA) corridor. These queries and keywords go strictly beyond basic price-per-sq-ft and standard RERA checks, capturing high-intent searches from distressed buyers, luxury investors, NRIs, and institutional operators.

## 1. Distressed Projects, Insolvency & Policy Resolution Mechanisms

These queries represent the massive search volume from buyers navigating legacy stalled projects, builder defaults, insolvency courts, and government bailouts.

### Top Distant Search Queries:
1. **"How does the Amitabh Kant Committee policy de-link flat registry from developer dues in Noida?"**
   * *Buyer Reality:* Homebuyers frantically search whether they can get their sub-lease deed registered when the builder owes hundreds of crores to Noida/GNIDA Authority. The UP Cabinet's 2023–2024 implementation allowed registries if developers paid 25% recalculated dues under the "Zero Period" formula.
2. **"What is the 'Zero Period' relief in Noida real estate, and does it reduce flat possession delays?"**
   * *Search Intent:* Understanding how the state government waives interest and penalties for builders during the COVID-19 pandemic (April 2020 – March 2022) and NGT litigation periods in exchange for committing to project completion timelines.
3. **"Can I buy a flat in an NCLT / CIRP (Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process) project, and what happens to my home loan?"**
   * *Financial Reality:* Inquiries regarding buying in projects like Jaypee Wish Town (Suraksha Group resolution) or Amrapali (NBCC takeover). Buyers ask whether nationalized banks (SBI, HDFC, PNB) sanction fresh home loans during insolvency.
4. **"What is the SWAMIH Investment Fund, and which stalled Noida societies received last-mile funding?"**
   * *Search Intent:* Investors and desperate allottees track which projects (e.g., in Sector 107, Sector 75, or Greater Noida West) got central government funding to finish civil structures.
5. **"What is the 100-homebuyer threshold rule under Section 7 of the IBC to drag a builder to NCLT?"**
   * *Legal Inquiry:* Buyers search for collective litigation prerequisites—requiring either 100 allottees or 10% of total project buyers to file an insolvency petition against a defaulting promoter.
6. **"What happens if UP RERA issues a Recovery Certificate (RC) against a developer?"**
   * *Procedural Reality:* Tracking how District Magistrate (DM) Gautam Buddha Nagar conducts recovery actions, seals developer sales offices, and auctions unsold inventory to refund buyer principal plus MCLR interest.

## 2. Geo-Hydrology, Floodplains & Environmental Hazards

High-intent queries from buyers investigating ecological vulnerabilities, flood risks, and environmental restrictions.

### Top Distant Search Queries:
1. **"Is property in the Hindon or Yamuna River 'Doob Kshetra' (floodplain) legal for residential registry?"**
   * *Legal Trap:* Heavy search volume following the August 2024 Allahabad High Court ruling on DM NOC requirements. While agricultural land transfer is technically permissible, any residential, commercial, or farmhouse construction is strictly illegal and subject to demolition by NGT and authority bulldozers.
2. **"Why are farmhouses in Noida Sector 135 and Shahberi facing demolition notices?"**
   * *Due Diligence:* Inquiries into unauthorized plotting on riverbeds, green buffers, and notified agricultural zones where buyers were lured by cheap prices and fake power connections.
3. **"How do winter GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan) Stage III and IV bans affect flat delivery dates?"**
   * *Construction Timeline Impact:* CAQM (Commission for Air Quality Management) orders ban non-essential construction, diesel generators, and earthwork from October to January, routinely triggering "force majeure" extension claims by builders under UP RERA.
4. **"What is the actual TDS and salinity level of groundwater in Sector 137, Sector 143, and Greater Noida West?"**
   * *Living Reality:* Residents search for borewell water quality (often 1,800 to 2,500+ ppm) causing plumbing corrosion, appliance breakdown, and hair loss before Ganga Jal supply is connected.
5. **"When will Stage-2 / Stage-3 Ganga Jal pipeline (85 cusecs / 375 MLD) reach my sector?"**
   * *Livability Tracking:* Specific infrastructure queries comparing sectors receiving 100% municipal surface water versus societies dependent entirely on deep borewells and private water tankers.
6. **"How close is my society to the Sector 123 waste-to-energy plant or the Ghazipur landfill smell zone?"**
   * *Livability Check:* Odor dispersion and prevailing wind direction queries affecting property rental values in Sector 120, 121, 122, and along the Hindon riverbank.

## 5. Post-Handover Living, RWA Governance & Society Economics

These queries are generated by actual homeowners and prospective buyers evaluating life *after* moving in.

### Top Distant Search Queries:
1. **"Under Section 14 of the UP Apartment Act 2010, when must the builder legally hand over maintenance to the AOA/RWA?"**
   * *Governance Clashes:* Homeowners search the statutory trigger—handover becomes mandatory once 60% of apartments are handed over or a Completion Certificate (CC) is obtained.
2. **"What is the UP RERA 12th Amendment rule regarding Interest-Free Maintenance Security (IFMS) transfer?"**
   * *Fund Recovery:* Searches regarding the July 2026 regulation requiring promoters to hold IFMS in separate high-yield fixed deposits and transfer 100% of the corpus plus accrued interest directly to the registered AOA.
3. **"When does 18% GST apply to society maintenance (CAM) charges in high-rises?"**
   * *Cost Stacking:* CBIC tax threshold queries: if monthly maintenance exceeds ₹7,500 per month per flat, does 18% GST apply to the entire amount or only the incremental amount? (Applies to the *entire* amount if it exceeds ₹7,500, unless the RWA's total annual turnover is below ₹20 Lakh).
4. **"Can an RWA legally ban pets from passenger lifts or restrict dog breeds in Noida?"**
   * *High-Rise Bylaws:* Allahabad High Court and Animal Welfare Board of India (AWBI) rulings nullifying arbitrary RWA pet bans, alongside Noida Authority's mandatory pet registration guidelines.
5. **"How do dual electricity meters work in Noida high-rises, and what are the separate PVVNL vs DG backup rates?"**
   * *Utility Calculations:* Understanding how the state electricity board (PVVNL) charges ₹6.50–₹7.50/unit on grid power, while automated dual-source meters bill DG backup power at ₹18–₹24/unit directly through the prepaid smart meter.
6. **"Can individual apartment owners install EV chargers in designated basement parking spots?"**
   * *EV Infrastructure:* Disputed bylaws between RWAs and residents regarding tapping power from personal meters versus centralized DISCOM charging feeders.
