### Top Industry Keywords &amp; Technical Jargon

Here is a breakdown of the defining terminology, search keywords, and legal jargon driving the real estate market in Noida, Greater Noida West, and the Yamuna Expressway corridor:

* **Authority Leasehold (90 or 99 Years)**: Land allotted across NOIDA, GNIDA, and YEIDA is held on a long-term leasehold basis where the local authority retains underlying land ownership while the buyer owns the structure[1][2].
* **Transfer Memorandum (TM) &amp; Transfer Charges**: Formal clearance issued by the local authority authorizing a property resale, incurring authority transfer fees typically ranging from 1% to 5% of the transaction or circular rate[3][4].
* **Landed Cost vs. Basic Sale Price (BSP)**: The total out-of-pocket cost paid by a buyer after adding stamp duty, registration, IFMS, club membership, lease rent, and taxes to the baseline per-square-foot price[5][6].
* **5% GST on Under-Construction vs. 0% on RTM**: Non-affordable under-construction flats attract 5% GST without input tax credit, whereas Ready-to-Move (RTM) units with an Occupancy Certificate carry 0% GST[5][6].
* **Occupancy Certificate (OC) &amp; Completion Certificate (CC)**: Statutory clearances issued by urban planning authorities confirming that a tower or phase is structurally complete and fit for human habitation[5].
* **UP RERA Registration (** **UPRERAPRJ...** **)**: The legal registration ID assigned under the Real Estate Regulatory Authority to enforce builder compliance and project timelines[5].
* **Carpet Area vs. Super Built-Up Area (Loading)**: Usable indoor floor space compared to total covered and shared area, with market loading typically ranging between 25% and 35%[6].
* **One-Time Lease Rent (10%)**: A single upfront payment equal to 10% of the land cost paid to the authority prior to lease deed execution to eliminate recurring annual ground rent[9][10].
* **Jewar Airport / YEIDA Corridor Alignment**: Location branding and valuation adjustments linked to the operational milestones of the Noida International Airport along the Yamuna Expressway[2].
* **Gated Societies (500+ Units / Group Housing)**: High-density residential developments built by established developers capable of sustaining integrated lifestyle amenities[12].
