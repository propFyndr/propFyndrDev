## 1. Tenancy Law, Co-Living & Commercial Homestays (Post-2021 Reforms)

These queries reflect the regulatory transformation of the rental economy, co-living sector, and residential conversion rules under updated Uttar Pradesh state legislation.

### Top Distant Search Queries:
1. **"Is online tenancy registration mandatory under the UP Urban Buildings Tenancy Act 2021, and what is the security deposit cap?"**
   * *Search Intent / Regulatory Reality:* Landlords and tenants search compliance requirements on the UP Tenancy Portal within 60 days of entering a lease. The Act strictly caps residential security deposits to **a maximum of 2 months' rent** (and 1 month for commercial premises) and establishes a dedicated Rent Tribunal, bypassing civil courts for eviction and dispute adjudication.
2. **"Can Noida Authority seal residential plots running commercial PGs or hostels in Sectors 62, 22, and 19?"**
   * *Niche Due Diligence:* Exploring authority enforcement notices against unauthorized commercial land use on residential plotted layouts. Plotted homeowners inquire about mixed-use compounding fees, mandatory fire safety clearances, commercial water/sewer tariffs, and parking mitigation penalties.
3. **"Can an apartment owner run an Airbnb or serviced homestay in a Noida high-rise under the UP Tourism Policy?"**
   * *Investor & Operator Intent:* Navigating clashes between RWA restrictive bylaws and the state tourism Bed & Breakfast/Homestay registration portal. Owners search for police verification rules, commercial electricity tariff implications, and legal precedents protecting private property leasing against RWA overreach.

## 2. Bank Distress, SARFAESI Auctions & Tripartite Loan Defaults

These queries capture high-risk asset acquisitions, bank foreclosure mechanics, and buyer protection failures during loan distress.

### Top Distant Search Queries:
1. **"Can I buy a bank-auctioned flat under the SARFAESI Act if the builder has unpaid Noida Authority land dues?"**
   * *High-Risk Auction Reality:* Bidders on IBAPI / e-auction platforms discover that winning a bank's foreclosure auction under Section 13(4) of the SARFAESI Act does *not* automatically clear underlying Noida/GNIDA Authority dues or transfer charges. The winning bidder often inherits developer liabilities before a sub-lease deed can be executed.
2. **"What legal recourse do homebuyers have when a builder defaults on an EMI-sharing Subvention Scheme?"**
   * *CIBIL & Insolvency Reality:* High-intent inquiries from buyers whose credit scores were damaged when developers stopped paying bank EMIs under "no EMI till possession" contracts, triggering bank recovery notices against individual buyers despite UP RERA directives holding promoters liable.
3. **"How does a Home Loan Balance Transfer (refinancing) work on a leasehold property when the original title deeds are in another bank's escrow?"**
   * *Financing Mechanics:* Navigating the NOC process, inter-bank title deed transfer protocols, and authority tripartite re-execution between lenders without triggering fresh stamp duties or lease rent re-assessments.

## 6. Inheritance, Succession, Will Probates & Family Settlement

These queries represent critical legal workflows for high-net-worth families, elderly allottees, and legal heirs navigating property succession in Uttar Pradesh.

### Top Distant Search Queries:
1. **"How do I transfer a deceased parent's Noida Authority leasehold flat onto legal heirs (Virasat / Warisan mutation)?"**
   * *Inheritance Due Diligence:* Steps for authority succession mutation—submitting Surviving Member Certificates (SMC), legal heir affidavits, registered family settlement deeds, and publication of 30-day public notices in national dailies.
2. **"Is probate of a Will mandatory for property in Noida, Greater Noida, or Ghaziabad?"**
   * *Succession Law:* Clarifying that under Section 213 of the Indian Succession Act, probate is mandatory in the original civil jurisdiction of presidency towns (Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai), but **not mandatory in Uttar Pradesh** unless the Will is challenged in high court litigation.
3. **"What is the stamp duty on a Relinquishment Deed (Haq-Tyag) between siblings for Noida property?"**
   * *Tax Shielding:* UP government amendments capping stamp duty on relinquishment/release deeds within blood relations to nominal fees (₹5,000 + registration) instead of standard 7% stamp duty.
